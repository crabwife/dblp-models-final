\textbf{Four-year distributional shape and temporal agreement of DBLP productivity models.} Empirical comparisons use 510 complete publication histories over career years 0--20; each model generates 10,000 histories. Panels A and B use the sum of annual adjusted productivity over the final four career years, 17--20, matching Shockley's four-year aggregation. (A) Normal QQ curves of positive four-year totals after taking $\log_2$ and standardizing each source separately to zero mean and unit sample standard deviation. The grey diagonal is the normal reference. Standardization removes location and scale; zero totals are excluded only from this panel and their counts are reported separately. QQ curves retain up to 401 ordered points, including the extremes; reported QQ $R^2$ uses all positive totals. (B) Exact empirical cumulative distribution functions of the same four-year totals, including zeros and full tails. The shared output axis is linear from zero to one and logarithmic above one. (C) Spearman correlation of annual productivity ranks five years apart, with average ranks for ties. The same complete empirical cohort is used at every year. Models share colors and line styles across panels. These are in-sample generative checks. The self-exciting hurdle model is fitted to complete histories; the other models use available adjacent-year observations. Whole-career QQ and ECDF views are retained in a separate supplement.
