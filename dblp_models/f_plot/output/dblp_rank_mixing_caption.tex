\textbf{Annual productivity rank mixing in DBLP.} The same 510 complete careers are used at every career year 0--20. $R_t$ denotes the cross-scholar rank of annual adjusted productivity at year $t$. Curves report Spearman $\rho_S(R_0,R_t)$, $\rho_S(R_{t-3},R_t)$ and $\rho_S(R_{t-5},R_t)$, starting at years 0, 3 and 5 respectively. Observed zeros are retained and ties receive average ranks. These curves describe annual ranks; no rolling or cumulative productivity is used.
