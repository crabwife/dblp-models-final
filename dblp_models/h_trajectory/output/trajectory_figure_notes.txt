Annual population curves compare the complete empirical cohort with model simulations. Raw and offset-log views use the same underlying values and ddof=0 variances. In secondary panels, shading marks years affected by the six-year empirical prefix of AR(6,6); other models generate from year 1. Annual-curve common-window scores use years 6--20; increment scores use destination years 7--20 so both endpoints are generated for all models. NRMSE is RMSE divided by the root mean square of the empirical curve over the same years. Path-feature panels use years 6--20 and connect compact ECDF knots; numerical distribution distances use all defined observations. Zero-output paths have undefined concentration and peak age and are excluded from those two descriptors only. Inactive runs are the longest observed within-window runs and may be truncated at boundaries; first maxima win ties. Population curves and unpaired distributions of path features describe different aspects of agreement, not predictions for identified researchers. All comparisons are in-sample and fit cohorts differ across models.
